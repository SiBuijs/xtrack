## Tilt sign (resolved)

- `THETA = −0.015` (`TiltedSolenoid`, 004a, 010a) is the beam's angle from
  the solenoid axis: B_x = −sin(`THETA`) B_s at the IP.
- The A_m formulas' θ_b is the solenoid axis' angle from the beam, so
  θ_b = −`THETA` = +0.015. That is what 001d/001e already used.
- Check: the 004a 2 T template has B_x(IP) = +0.02983 T, and this code
  reproduces it exactly. IDEA has B_x(0) = +0.037 T, an edge peak of
  −0.195 T and an opposite-sign peak of +0.100 T at 2.035 m. 010a has the
  same signs: +0.045 / −0.188 / +0.095 T.
- The user had agreed to switch to −0.015, on my wrong premise that θ_b =
  `THETA`. The check showed that would flip B_x against every ring model,
  so `idea_solenoid_field.TILT_ANGLE = −THETA` instead.

## Field model: exact coefficients, not O(θ²)

- **001d's A_0 is wrong.** Its θ² term, θ²(s b/2 + s² b′/4), is
  dimensionally inconsistent.
  - Rederived: A_0 = b − θ²(b/2 + s b′ + s² b″/4).
  - Against `TiltedSolenoid` (analytic 2 T): 5.05 mT error with 001d's form,
    0.058 mT rederived.
  - Fixed in `idea_solenoid_field.beam_frame_from_derivatives` (001e
    figures); 001d itself is untouched.
- **O(θ²) is not enough at the IDEA edge.**
  - A_1's O(θ³) remainder is 0.54 mT for the analytic 2 T solenoid and
    ~2.5 mT for IDEA. On the beam axis r = sθ ≈ 2 cm, which is not small
    against the edge width.
  - With the O(θ²) A_m, the BFE line was 20 mT (B_s) and 2.5 mT (B_x) off
    the exact field.
- **Exact construction** (`exact_beam_frame_polynomials`):
  - Inside a spline piece b is a quartic, so the paraxial series terminates
    (Bz′ = b − r²b″/4 + r⁴b⁗/64, Br = −r b′/2 + r³b‴/16).
  - With z′ = x sinθ_b + s cosθ_b and x′ = x cosθ_b − s sinθ_b linear, the
    beam-frame field is a polynomial in (x, y, s), computed by bivariate
    polynomial algebra.
  - The element stores A_0 → `ksolc` and ∂ⁱB_x/∂xⁱ, i = 0..4 → `ksc` (rows
    0..4, as the 004a templates). `knc` = 0, since B_y = 0 on y = 0.
  - The piece used is the one at the element's midpoint. On the axis the
    knot sits up to 1.1e-4·s (0.26 mm at 2.3 m) inside the element.
- **Checks** (004a_build_idea_solenoid output):

  | check | result |
  |---|---|
  | analytic 2 T, this code vs `TiltedSolenoid` / 004a template | ≤ 7 µT on axis, ≤ 24 µT at 5 mm (6 mm profile fit; with 2.4 cm elements the fit's zero end condition gave 0.8 mT) |
  | IDEA BFE line vs exact tilted field, \|x\|, \|y\| ≤ 1 cm | < 0.01 µT more than 0.3 mm from a knot; up to 57 µT inside the slivers, where the two switch pieces on planes tilted by θ |
  | tracking vs `BorisSpatialIntegrator` through the exact field | 3.4e-11 (was 8.2e-9 with O(θ²)) |
  | RK4 steps per 0.1 m element, vs 64 | 1: 6.0e-8, 2: 3.8e-9, 4: 2.4e-10, **8: 1.5e-11 (default)**, 16: 9.1e-13 |
  | symplecticity \|RᵀSR − S\| | 1.4e-8, the same at 4, 8 and 16 steps |
  | ∫B_s | 4.656880 T·m, line = spline |

- **Symplecticity.** The 1.4e-8 is not the integrator. The C3 profile's
  b⁗ jumps at the knots, so the off-axis field jumps by up to ~1 mT at
  x = y = 5 mm at element boundaries. With `pkin_const` that gives
  ΔA/Bρ ~ 1e-8. Moving the boundaries to t_k / cosθ changed nothing. At
  ~1e-8 per pass it is far below radiation damping (~1e-3 per turn at the
  Z).

## Line and correctors

- **Main line:** 54 elements over |s| ≤ z_m = 2.30 m: 46 of 0.1 m, plus
  splits at the corrector segments (0.4–10 cm). The free drift between each
  IP and QD0 is 2.400 m at all four IPs. With taper 0.5 m, edges up to
  1.50 m fit (z_m ≤ 2.40 m); 1.55 and 1.60 m do not.
- **First corrector (010a-style):**
  - 010a's segments 1.78–1.965 / 2.015–2.2 m (weights 1.78 : 1) cover its
    opposite-sign B_x lobe, 1.758–2.283 m at > 5 % of the peak (computed
    from 010a's fields).
  - Mapped affinely onto the IDEA lobe (1.882–2.228 m) they become
    1.896–2.018 and 2.051–2.173 m, with the same weights.
  - Knobs `acb{h,v}1_seg{n}_sol_{side}_{ip}` = weight × `acb{h,v}1_sol_
    {side}_{ip}`. 010a has only the vertical one; the horizontal one uses the
    same segments because 004c varies both.
  - 010a's weight 1.7799 is not explained anywhere in the repo.
- **Other correctors:** 004c, unchanged: `acb*2–5` on QD0A/QD0B/QF1A/QF1B,
  `acb*6` on `corr_sol` (±11.95 m), doublet rotation, optics trims including
  the mid-bend ones, and the skew coupling quads.
- **Compensation solenoids:** the 004a 2 T template ×0.9486, so that main
  + 2 × compensation ∫B_s = 0 (−2.328 T·m each; ring total 5e-14 T·m).
- **Installed ring, solenoids off:** ΔQy = −1.1e-7, IP β-beat 5e-7 against
  the bare ring. The SB 2 T and BNL-map lattices show the same order
  (−1.4e-7 and −4.0e-7), so this is not specific to IDEA.
