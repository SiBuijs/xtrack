# SplineBoris → BFieldExpansion, phase 1: linear optics (2026-11-03)

Branch `bfieldexpansion_refactor`. **Exploratory**: the paper's numbers stay on
SplineBoris (`fcc_nonlocal_paper`), nothing here goes into `paper/RESULTS.md`
until we decide to switch. Plan: `~/.claude/plans/hello-i-d-like-to-rosy-aho.md`.

**Verdict up front.**
- On the same lattice, `xt.BFieldExpansion` (BFE) reproduces SplineBoris (SB)
  to SB's own convergence level. The remaining SB-vs-BFE differences in the
  corrected rings come from **004c landing on a slightly different knob
  solution**, not from the element model.
- BFE at 2 RK4 steps per slice costs about as much as SB at 10 Boris steps
  and is ~15× more accurate.
- SB at its production 10 steps carries a small step error of its own
  (3T corrected ring: Q″y 4668.487 vs converged 4668.448, β ≤ 9e-6).

## Decisions (user, 2026-11-03)

- **Scope:** FCC study only. The library's `xt.SplineBoris` stays.
- **Not yet supported (at the time):** radiation and spin. BFE had neither,
  so 009/010/013/014/015/018 stayed on SB. *Update 2026-11-05:* radiation and
  spin were ported from `bfe_radiation_spin` to `fcc_nonlocal_paper` (both
  integrators), with the cases `bfe_on` (RK4) and `bfeboris_on` (Boris) in
  009/010/018.
- **Momenta:** `pkin_const=True`, the same kinetic-momentum convention as SB,
  so 004c's W targets and note 11 stay valid.
- **Coexisting models:** BFE is a third model next to SB and VarSol:
  `--model bfe` (analytic) and `--model partialmap_bfe` (the BNL map).
- **Nominal field only:** BFE multiplies `knl/ksl` by `kscale`, while SB's
  `scale_b` does not. The in-solenoid correctors (`acbh1/acbv1_sol_*`) sit
  on the main slices as `knl[0]/ksl[0]`, so BFE matches SB only at
  `on_sol = main_b_scale = 1`. `bfe_setup.check_bfe_nominal_field(line)`
  raises otherwise. 004c (before saving) and 004n (every state) call it.
  `main_b_scale` scans and solenoid-off cases with live correctors stay on SB.

## How the conversion works (`bfe_setup.py`)

`splineboris_to_bfe` converts already-built SB slices, so the analytic
templates and the `LongitudinalFitter` map line both work without changing
`xtrack/`:
- each Spline4 row goes through `hermite_to_polynomial` (in
  `splineboris_src/spline_B_field_eval_python.py`) and is divided by Bρ;
- the coefficients map as `bx → ksc`, `by → knc`, `bs → ksolc`, with the same
  derivative convention and no factorials;
- `scale_b → kscale`, and `knl/ksl` get one allocated entry for the correctors.

004b converts the 004a templates on the fly, so no BFE templates are stored.
The BFE lattices (~14 MB each) are gitignored and regenerated by
004b/004c (`--model bfe`).

## Element and installed-ring checks

Checks on the elements and the installed ring:
- **Field, per slice:** SB vs BFE agree to ≤ 2e-15 T for |x|, |y| ≤ 1 cm,
  for 2T, 3T, 3T o2 and both solenoid types.
- **Converged exits:** SB vs BFE ≤ 4e-14 (`bfe_check_conversion.py`).
- **Step convergence, main 2T solenoid:**

  | | max exit error | time per pass |
  |---|---|---|
  | SB, 10 steps | 7.1e-12 | 3.5 ms |
  | BFE, 1 step | 7.3e-12 | 1.9 ms |
  | BFE, 2 steps (`DEFAULT_BFE_STEPS`) | 4.6e-13 | 3.3 ms |

- **BNL map path:** SB and BFE differ by ~1e-12 against Boris through the raw
  map, where the fit error is ~1e-7. The symplecticity error of the map line
  is 3.1e-11 for BFE vs 2.7e-10 for SB.
- **Solenoid offset:** SB's `shift_x/shift_y` is a field offset; BFE uses the
  standard frame shift. For a translation they agree to ~1e-14, while the
  effect of the shift itself is 1e-8.
- **Uncorrected 2T ring**, |C⁻| = 0.11, compared with BFE at 8 steps:

  | | ΔQ | max Δβy/βy |
  |---|---|---|
  | SB, 10 steps | ~2.4e-7 / 3.9e-7 | 7.5e-4 |
  | SB, 100 steps | ~2.6e-9 | 7.5e-6 |
  | BFE, 2 steps | ~1e-8 | 5.9e-7 |

  This difference is SB step error, which falls 100× for 10× the steps
  (second order).
- **Regression:** the SB outputs of both 004b scripts are identical to the
  committed lattices, up to the order of the `sext_amp` expressions.

## Where the 3T Q″y difference (−6.0) comes from

`bfe_check_q2_steps.py` (log `data/logs/q2steps_*_3T.log`): each corrected
lattice is converged in its own step count, but to a different value:

| corrected 3T lattice | Q″y at the listed steps per slice |
|---|---|
| SB | 4668.487 (10), 4668.448 (40), 4668.448 (160) |
| BFE | 4662.525 (1), 4662.510 (2), 4662.509 (8) |

`bfe_check_swap_same_lattice.py` takes the SB-corrected 3T ring with frozen
knobs and swaps every SB slice for its BFE twin:

| | Q′y | Q″y |
|---|---|---|
| SB slices, 10 steps | 5.636170 | 4668.487 |
| BFE slices, 2 steps | 5.636183 | 4668.447 |
| SB slices, 160 steps | 5.636183 | 4668.448 |

The β difference between the BFE run and the SB 10-step run is ≤ 9e-6.
**On the same lattice the models agree.** The difference is in the 004c
solutions:
- 1104 knob values differ;
- the small optics-trim quads differ most, by O(100%), e.g.
  `k1_qd0ar.2_sol_corr` −4.2e-6 (SB) vs +2.2e-5 (BFE);
- the skew quads (`ks…`) differ by a median of 2.5e-5 relative, the
  orbit correctors (`acb…`) by up to 3e-2.

This fits note 10. The optics match keeps near-null directions even at
`OPTICS_RCOND = 1e-6`, and a ~1e-7 change in the solenoid map moves the
solution along them without changing any target. Q″y (not a target) moves
with the QD0→sdy1 phase at ~2.4e7 per unit tune, so a few units correspond
to Δμ ~ 1e-7. The phase difference itself was not measured here.

**Implication for the paper (not acted on):** at 3T, the corrected-ring Q″y
is reproducible only to about ±6 (0.13%) under a 1e-7-level change of the
solenoid map; at 2T, to ~1 (0.07%). That is an uncertainty of the correction
procedure, not of the element. It is worth a sentence if we quote Q″y to more
than 3 significant figures.

## Caveats found on the way

- **BFE is silent on spin in line tracking.** With
  `configure_spin('auto')`, a BFE slice leaves the spin unchanged and raises
  no error. `BFieldExpansion.track` called directly does raise, but the check
  in `xtrack/tracker.py:304` is commented out. In the same test SB rotated
  `spin_y` by 3.1e-4. So polarization from a BFE lattice (004d
  `polarization_analysis`, 015, 018) would be silently wrong.
- **BFE does not radiate.** 004n stores an `emittance_note` for the BFE
  models: the Chao emittances, energy loss and damping exclude the
  solenoids' own radiation, so they are not comparable with SB.
- **Line table:** BFE reports its integrated field in `knl/ksl` (so `tw.k0l`
  etc. include the solenoid content; SB contributes 0), and the table's `bs`
  column exists for SB only. 004b computes Bs via
  `bfe_setup.bs_mean_tesla`.
- **004d is not ported:** it needs spin, radiation and `tw.bs`.
