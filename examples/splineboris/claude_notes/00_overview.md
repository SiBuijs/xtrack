## The field-map pipeline (current)

```
raw map: pd.DataFrame, MultiIndex ('X','Y','Z'), columns ('Bx','By','Bs')
   |
   |  stage 1: TubeFitter            xtrack/_temp/splineboris/tube_fitter.py
   |    tube approach (Riemann & Aiba, IPAC2021 TUPAB238): one scalar
   |    potential  sum Psi[j,p,q] x^p y^q beta_j(z)  over all map points,
   |    tent (degree-1) basis in z, block-tridiagonal normal equations
   |    -> tf.on_axis_multipoles() = (z_frames, F, names)
   |    -> tf.on_axis_bs()         = the map's own on-axis Bs on its planes
   v
   |  stage 2: LongitudinalFitter    xtrack/_temp/splineboris/longitudinal_fitter.py
   |    per component, least-squares C3 quartic B-spline on uniform nodes
   |    LongitudinalFitter(s_start, s_end, n_elements | points_per_element,
   |                       end_condition="zero"|"free", preserve_integral, period)
   |    lf.fit(z, F, names);  lf.fit(*tf.on_axis_bs(), [("Bs", 0)])
   v
   lf.to_line(multipole_order, steps_per_point, field_tol, r_ref, shift_x, ...)
       -> xt.Line of SplineBoris "splineboris_NNN", one per knot interval
   lf.to_multipole_line(p0c, multipole_order, field_at="mean"|"midpoint", ...)
       -> xt.Line of thick Multipole "multipole_NNN" on the same nodes, no Bs
```

- **Component names** everywhere: `("By", n) = d^n By/dx^n (0,0,s)`,
  `("Bx", n) = d^n Bx/dx^n (0,0,s)`, `("Bs", 0)`.
- **Per element** each component is a `Spline4` with five numbers
  `(f(s_k), f'(s_k), f(s_k+1), f'(s_k+1), mean_k)`
  (`LongitudinalFitter.element_params(name)`).
- **Only the q=0/q=1 rows of `Psi` are passed on.** The Van der Schueren /
  "Table-1" evaluator regenerates all higher transverse structure from the
  on-axis components, Maxwell-consistent by construction. That evaluator is
  `evaluate_B` in `xtrack/beam_elements/splineboris_src/`
  (`spline_B_field_eval_python.py` plus C generated by
  `_generate_bpmeth_to_C.py`). The rationale is in
  `tube_schueren_integration.md`; `TubeFitter.check_trace_consistency()` is
  the diagnostic for that assumption.
- **`n_frames` vs `residual_tol`** (mutually exclusive): fixed frame count
  (default `DEFAULT_N_FRAMES = 200`), or a search for the smallest `n_frames`
  that meets a relative residual, with the trace plotted by
  `plot_n_frames_search()`. One frame per map plane avoids tent smoothing.
- **`field_tol` + `r_ref` are export-time arguments** of
  `to_line`/`to_multipole_line`. A component is dropped if
  `max|f| r_ref^n / n!` is below `field_tol` times the largest der-0 field.
  (In the old code `field_tol` was a fitter argument.)
- **End conditions:** `"zero"` (f..f''' = 0 at both ends; for solenoid maps
  that are field-free at the ends) or `"free"` (SLS undulator maps, which are
  not). `period=` only warns if there are fewer than 12 elements per period.
- **The element itself:** `xt.SplineBoris`/`Spline4` in
  `xtrack/beam_elements/splineboris.py` (not `elements.py`). Examples
  001/002 show the bare API.

What was removed, and when: TubeFitter's kernel options in `1666b0eb4`
(2026-08-14, tent-only since then); then, in the 2026-10-08 rewrite
(`0f77ee734`, `a8e5ecc17`, after TubeFitter simplifications on 10-06),
TubeFitter's Hermite/region export, `df_fit_pars` and
`TubeFitter.to_line()`/`to_multipole_line()`, then `FieldFitter`
(`field_fitter.py`), `SplineBorisSequence` (`splineboris_sequence.py`),
the `*_fit_pars*.csv` test fixtures, and the examples `003a_fieldfitter_basic_usage.py`,
`003b_tube_fitter_basic_usage.py` and `003c_fieldfitter_vs_tubefitter.py`.

## Example scripts (as of 2026-10-14)

- `001_splineboris_api_intro.py`, `002_homogeneous_rbend.py` — bare
  `SplineBoris` API; constant field vs `RBend`.
- `003a_tube_fitter_basic_usage.py` / `003b_longitudinal_fitter_basic_usage.py`
  — the two stages on the SLS map, with knobs to play with.
- `003c_accuracy_scan.py` — field error of the exported elements against the
  raw map vs `n_frames` (stage 1) and element length (stage 2). This is the
  tool for any "how accurate is the fit" question.
- `003d_tube_fitter_nframes_scan.py` — `residual_tol` search for the
  smallest `n_frames`.
- `004a_build_undulator.py`, `004b_undulators_in_sls_ring.py`,
  `004c_c_minus_vs_offset.py` — build the SLS undulator, put it in the ring,
  and C^- vs horizontal offset.
- `006_solenoid.py` / `006a_solenoid.py` — analytic `SolenoidField` map,
  fit and tracking. 006 is a 2.6 m, 3 T, a = 0.13 solenoid with
  multipole_order 4; 006a is 4 m, 1.5 T, a = 0.3 with multipole_order 2.
  Both use `energy0=45.6e6` (eV, i.e. 45.6 MeV), although 006a's comment
  says GeV.
- `006b_fcc_ee_solenoid.py` — the BNL 2 T FCC-ee detector-solenoid map
  (`test_data/fcc_ee_solenoids/Field_map_2T_BNL_August_14_2026.csv`, one side
  of the IP, ±10 mm grid), tracked at 45.6 GeV vs Boris through the raw map.
- `007a_sls_undulator_tune_displacement.py`,
  `007b_sls_undulator_tune_displacement_local_optics.py`,
  `007c_undulator_focusing_decomposition.py` — tune shift vs undulator offset,
  SB vs MK vs the X11MA measurement (notes below; 007c is the term-by-term
  K_x/K_y decomposition on the wiggling orbit, new 2026-10-13, no note).
- `008_undulator_open_track_spin_radiation.py`,
  `009_sls_undulators_closed_spin_radiation.py` — spin + radiation, open
  line and closed ring.
- `010_solenoid_tubefitter_comparison.py` — analytic solenoid, coarse vs fine
  elements, against `BorisSpatialIntegrator` as reference.
- `012_sls_undulator_spin_emittance_evolution.py` — 7-case spin/emittance
  tracking (note below).

Caches: `data/` (007a, also read by 007b) and `data_007b/` are gitignored. A
cache written before 2026-10-08 came from the old fitter.

Tests: `tests/test_splineboris.py`, with fixtures `undulator_raw_data`,
`undulator_fit`, `solenoid_fit` (TubeFitter + LongitudinalFitter), and tests
for the `LongitudinalFitter` (C3 continuity, free ends, integral
preservation, `to_multipole_line`, `field_tol`), the TubeFitter
`n_frames`/`residual_tol` logic, and the element physics (homogeneous field,
radiation, spin, `scale_b`, backtracking).
